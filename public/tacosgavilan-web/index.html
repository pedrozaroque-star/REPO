Our Story

Since 1992, Tacos Gavilan has been bringing the authentic taste of Mexico to Los Angeles. What started as a single taqueria has grown into 15 locations serving thousands of families every day.

We pride ourselves on using traditional recipes and cooking with passion. From our al pastor to our signature salsas, every meal reflects decades of culinary dedication.

2. Contact Form Inquiries

When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), nearest store selection, topic, and message solely to respond to your inquiry and prevent automated abuse.

Contact messages are retained according to operational and legal needs and then deleted or de-identified. Tacos Gavilan must approve a specific retention schedule before one is promised publicly.

This website is not used to sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. It does not use advertising cookies. Google Maps loads only after you request the interactive locator; Toast processes orders on its own website.
