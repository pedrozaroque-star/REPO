Privacy Policy & California Privacy Notice (CCPA/CPRA)

Effective Date: March 19, 2019 · Last Updated: October 16, 2026. Tacos Gavilan, LLC and its affiliated operating entities ("Tacos Gavilan") explain here how we collect, use, protect, and disclose information on https://tacosgavilan.com and how California residents can exercise their privacy rights.

1. Scope & Corporate Entity

Tacos Gavilan, LLC and its affiliated operating entities (collectively, "Tacos Gavilan," "we," "us," or "our") operate the https://tacosgavilan.com website (the "Service") and our 15 Southern California restaurant locations. This Privacy Policy & California Notice at Collection informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, retention, protection, and disclosure of Personal Data when you use our Service and the rights and choices you have associated with that data under applicable United States and California privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA / CPRA, Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.).

2. Types of Data Collected (California Notice at Collection)

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Data Voluntarily Provided (Contact, Newsletter & Careers): When you submit an inquiry through our Contact Form, subscribe to updates, or submit an employment inquiry, we collect your full name, email address, telephone number (optional or required by form), preferred restaurant location, inquiry category, and message content solely to respond to your request, provide guest support, or evaluate your inquiry.
  • Usage Data & Technical Server Logs: When you access the Service, our hosting and security infrastructure automatically processes standard technical information sent by your browser ("Usage Data"), including your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, pages visited, date and time of your visit, time spent on those pages, referring URLs, and diagnostic logs to deliver the website and defend against automated abuse.
  • Location Data ("Find Me" Store Locator): If you click the optional "Find Me" / "Use My Location" button on our interactive store locator map, we request permission from your browser to access your approximate or precise geographic location solely to calculate distances to our 15 restaurant locations in real time within your browser. We do not persistently store, profile, or sell your GPS coordinates on our servers, and you can enable or revoke location permissions at any time through your device or browser settings.
  • Payment & Financial Data: Tacos Gavilan does NOT collect, process, or store credit card, debit card, or banking numbers on this website. All online food orders and payments are processed directly by independent PCI-DSS compliant ordering platforms such as Toast POS (order.online) or third-party delivery marketplaces.

3. Tracking Technologies, Cookies & Google Tag Manager

We use cookies and similar storage technologies (such as browser localStorage) to operate our website, remember your preferences, analyze traffic, and measure marketing campaigns:

  • Strictly Necessary / Preference Cookies: Used to remember your chosen language (tacosgavilan_lang), your ADA accessibility adjustments (tg_a11y_prefs), and your California privacy/cookie consent choices (tg_cookie_consent).
  • Analytics & Performance Technologies: With your consent (unless you opt out), we use Google Tag Manager (GTM-WW2N58Z) and Google Analytics to understand aggregate website traffic, page interactions, and outbound clicks to ordering platforms so we can improve site speed and guest experience.
  • Advertising & Behavioral Remarketing Tags: Unless disabled by you or blocked by a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, our Google Tag Manager container may deploy conversion linker and remarketing tags (such as Google Ads Conversion Tracking _gcl, Meta/Facebook Pixel, or TikTok Pixel) to measure advertising effectiveness and display relevant Tacos Gavilan promotions across external networks.

How to Control or Opt Out of Cookies & Trackers: You can manage or disable non-essential analytics and marketing cookies at any time with one click via our California Privacy Choices page or by clicking "Privacy Choices" / "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" in our website footer. When you opt out, our site enforces Google Consent Mode v2 (setting ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization, and analytics_storage to denied) and purges non-essential tracking cookies.

4. How We Use Your Data

Tacos Gavilan uses the collected data for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our website, menus, and store locator;
  • To respond to your customer care inquiries, catering questions, and feedback;
  • To notify you about changes to our restaurants, hours, or services when requested;
  • To monitor the usage and performance of our Service and detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or security incidents;
  • To comply with applicable federal, state, and local legal obligations.

5. Data Retention & Security of Data

Retention of Data: Tacos Gavilan retains your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. Contact form messages and guest inquiries are retained only as long as needed to resolve your inquiry, maintain quality assurance records, or comply with legal, tax, or regulatory obligations, after which they are securely deleted or de-identified. Usage Data is retained for internal diagnostic and security analysis for shorter durations unless required to strengthen security or comply with legal obligations.

Transfer & Security of Data: Your information is processed and maintained on secure servers located in the United States. We implement industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards—including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, row-level database security, strict input sanitization, and rate limiting. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and while we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Service Providers, Third-Party Links & Disclosure of Data

Service Providers: We employ trusted third-party companies to facilitate our Service ("Service Providers"), including Vercel (cloud web hosting), Supabase (secure database storage for contact inquiries), Google (Google Maps API for our interactive store locator, Google Fonts, and Google Analytics/Ads where consented), and Toast POS / delivery platforms (DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, Postmates) when you choose to place an online food order. These third parties have access only to the data needed to perform their specific tasks and are obligated not to disclose or use it for unauthorized purposes.

Links to Other Sites: Our Service contains links to external ordering and social media sites not operated by us. If you click on a third-party link (such as order.online, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, Postmates, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, or YouTube), you will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit, as we have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content or privacy practices of third-party websites.

Business Transactions, Law Enforcement & Legal Requirements: If Tacos Gavilan is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your Personal Data may be transferred subject to continued privacy protections. Under certain circumstances, Tacos Gavilan may also disclose your Personal Data for law enforcement purposes if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or government agency), or in the good-faith belief that such action is necessary to: (a) comply with a legal obligation; (b) protect and defend the rights or property of Tacos Gavilan; (c) prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the Service; (d) protect the personal safety of users of the Service or the public; or (e) protect against legal liability.

7. Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA) & Global Privacy Control

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) grant you specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Know & Access: You have the right to request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, the business purpose for collecting it, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request the deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to statutory exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: You have the right to request that we correct inaccurate personal information maintained about you.
  • Right to Opt Out of "Sale" or "Sharing" (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.135): Tacos Gavilan never sells your personal information for monetary consideration. Because California law defines "sharing" to include disclosing online identifiers to third-party advertising networks via cookies or pixels for cross-context behavioral advertising, you have the right to opt out at any time via our Your Privacy Choices page or by broadcasting a Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal (navigator.globalPrivacyControl), which our website honors automatically as a valid opt-out.
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information & Non-Discrimination: We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes other than providing the service you reasonably expect, and we will never discriminate against you for exercising any CCPA/CPRA rights.

How to Submit a Verifiable Consumer Request: To exercise your rights to Know, Delete, or Correct, please email us at info@tacosgavilan.com or submit a request through our Online Contact Form. To protect your privacy, we will verify your identity by matching the email address or contact details provided in your request with the records we maintain before fulfilling the request. You may also designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf by providing written authorization.

8. Children's Privacy (Under Age 18 / COPPA)

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 18 ("Children"), and we do not knowingly collect, sell, or share personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 18 (or under 16/13 under CCPA and COPPA). If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us immediately at info@tacosgavilan.com. If we become aware that we have collected Personal Data from a minor without verification of parental consent, we take immediate steps to remove that information from our servers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective and last updated" date at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
