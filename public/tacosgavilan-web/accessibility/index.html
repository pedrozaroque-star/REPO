Accessibility Statement (ADA & California Compliance)

Effective Date: April 15, 2019 · Last Updated: October 16, 2026. Tacos Gavilan is committed to facilitating digital accessibility and usability for all guests, including individuals with disabilities.

1. General Commitment to Digital Accessibility

Tacos Gavilan strives to ensure that its digital services and restaurants are welcoming and accessible to all guests, including individuals with disabilities. Tacos Gavilan has invested significant resources to help ensure that https://tacosgavilan.com is made easier to use and more accessible for people with disabilities, with the strong belief that every person has the right to live with dignity, equality, comfort, and independence.

2. Accessibility Standards & Conformance Status (WCAG 2.1 / 2.2 Level AA)

To fulfill this commitment, Tacos Gavilan designs, audits, and maintains its website in alignment with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 and 2.2 Level AA published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), and the California Unruh Civil Rights Act (Cal. Civ. Code § 51 / AB 1757).

3. Accessibility Features & Assistive Toolbar on TacosGavilan.com

Our website incorporates both native semantic accessibility engineering and a dedicated ADA Accessibility Toolbar (available via the floating accessibility icon on every page):

  • Semantic HTML5 & Keyboard Navigation: Full support for keyboard-only navigation, visible focus rings, a "Skip to main content" link, ARIA landmarks, and keyboard focus traps with Escape key dismissal across all modals and drawers.
  • Vision & Contrast Adjustments: High Contrast mode, Color Inversion, Monochrome/Grayscale mode, and WCAG AA compliant color contrast ratios.
  • Blindness & Audio Screen Reading: Built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) click-to-speak reader and Full Page Audio Reader in both English and Spanish, alongside descriptive alt attributes on menu and restaurant photographs.
  • Reading, Typography & Motion Controls: Multi-step text size scaling, Dyslexia-friendly font mode, increased text spacing, link highlighting, enlarged cursor, reading guide ruler, and one-click animation pause (honoring prefers-reduced-motion).

4. Disclaimer & Ongoing Accessibility Efforts

Tacos Gavilan continues its efforts to constantly improve the accessibility of its site and services in the belief that it is our collective moral obligation to allow seamless, accessible, and unhindered use also for those of us with disabilities. Despite our efforts to make all pages and content on https://tacosgavilan.com fully accessible, some content or third-party integrations (such as external online ordering portals on order.online, delivery marketplaces, or third-party interactive map tiles) may not have yet been fully adapted to the strictest accessibility standards or are controlled by external providers.

5. Here For You — Personal & Telephone Assistance

If you are experiencing difficulty with any content on https://tacosgavilan.com or require assistance with any part of our site, reviewing our menu, or placing an order, please contact us during normal business hours and we will be happy to assist you. You may also call any of our 15 Southern California restaurant locations directly, where our team will gladly read menu options and take your order by telephone under the exact same pricing and terms.
