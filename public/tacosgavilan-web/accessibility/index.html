Accessibility Statement

Effective Date: October 16, 2026  ·  Last Updated: November 21, 2026  ·  Last Technical Accessibility Review: November 21, 2026  ·  Version: 3.0  ·  Operator: Tacos Gavilan

Tacos Gavilan is committed to full and equal enjoyment across our 15 Southern California restaurants and digital services pursuant to Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and the California Unruh Civil Rights Act, actively working toward WCAG 2.1 Level AA and WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance.

1. Our Commitment, California Legal Framework & Conformance Goal

Tacos Gavilan is committed to facilitating digital usability, independence, and equal access to our website (https://tacosgavilan.com) and restaurant services for all guests, including individuals with visual, auditory, motor, speech, or cognitive disabilities, in alignment with Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (42 U.S.C. § 12182; 28 C.F.R. Part 36), the California Unruh Civil Rights Act (Cal. Civ. Code § 51), and the California Disabled Persons Act (Cal. Civ. Code §§ 54–54.1).

Technical Standard & Transparent Conformance Status: Tacos Gavilan targets and actively works toward Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance (incorporating all WCAG 2.1 Level AA criteria recognized by the U.S. Department of Justice and California courts, plus WCAG 2.2 enhancements for focus visibility, target sizing, and authentication). Because web content, assistive technologies, and independent third-party ordering or map platforms evolve continuously, we classify our digital ecosystem as partially conformant and undergoing continuous improvement. In accordance with Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidance, we never claim that any automated script or toolbar guarantees a 100% error-free website, and we actively welcome guest feedback to remediate any barrier promptly.

2. Scope, Native HTML5/ARIA Engineering & Hybrid Testing Methodology

This Accessibility Statement applies to all first-party pages of tacosgavilan.com (including our Homepage, Menu, Fiesta Platter, Locations directory, all 15 individual Restaurant Location pages, Blog, Contact page, and Legal pages). Our primary accessibility foundation is engineered directly into the native HTML5, CSS, and JavaScript source code:

  • Semantic HTML5 Landmarks & Heading Hierarchy: Pages are structured with native landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main id="main-content">, <footer>), a single primary <h1> per page, and a keyboard-accessible "Skip to main content" link.
  • Keyboard Navigation, Focus Traps & Mobile Navigation Drawer: Interactive dialogs, modals (Map Modal, Dish Lightbox, Cookie Preference Center, Accessibility Panel, Statement Modals), and the Mobile Navigation Drawer (#mobile-navigation) maintain synchronized aria-expanded, aria-controls, and aria-hidden states, trap Tab / Shift + Tab focus while open, close on the Escape key, and restore focus to the triggering control.
  • Accessible Forms & Live Status Announcements: Contact and privacy forms use explicit <label for="..."> associations, aria-required="true", field-level aria-invalid states, and polite live regions (role="status" aria-live="polite") so screen readers announce validation messages and Reference ID confirmations.
  • Touch Target Sizing & Responsive Reflow: Primary interactive controls, navigation toggles, language switches, and close buttons meet WCAG 2.1 / 2.2 target-size guidance (44×44px minimum on primary controls) and reflow cleanly from 320px mobile viewports through desktop monitors without horizontal text clipping.
  • Accessible Text Alternatives for Maps, Menus & Store Cards: Food and restaurant photographs include descriptive alt attributes (while decorative icons carry aria-hidden="true"), all menu items are rendered in native HTML text (never inaccessible scanned PDFs), and all 15 restaurant addresses, cross-streets (including Los Angeles (Vernon & Broadway), Los Angeles (Central & Washington), and Los Angeles (Slauson & Broadway)), hours, and clickable tel: links are provided in accessible HTML cards alongside our map.
  • No-JavaScript Progressive Enhancement & Hybrid Audits: All legal notices (/privacy/, /terms/, /accessibility/, /privacy-choices/) and store directory pages render complete, readable HTML even if JavaScript is disabled. We evaluate our code through a hybrid methodology combining automated ruleset scanning (axe-core and Google Lighthouse) with manual keyboard-only testing and screen-reader checks using NVDA (Windows), Apple VoiceOver (macOS/iOS), and Google TalkBack (Android).

3. Supplementary Accessibility Toolbar & Kore AI Neural Voice (Alt + A)

As a free, optional assistive supplement layered on top of our native HTML5/ARIA engineering (and never as a replacement for source-code accessibility), our Site provides a bilingual (English / Español) Accessibility Panel accessible via the floating accessibility button (dockable to the Left or Right side of the viewport) or the Alt + A keyboard shortcut:

  • Bilingual Kore AI Neural Female Voice Reader (English & Español): Includes an interactive Click / Tab Reader mode (reads menu items, USD prices, headings, buttons, and store cards aloud as you navigate) and a Read Full Page mode with section-by-section skip controls, a floating mini audio player bar, pitch-preserved playback speeds (0.85x, 1x, 1.15x, 1.3x), and automatic offline female browser-voice fallback.
  • 6 Smart 1-Click Accessibility Profiles: Instant preset combinations tailored for Blind & Kore Voice, Low Vision, Dyslexia Friendly, ADHD Focus, Motor & Keyboard, and Seizure Safe browsing.
  • Granular Multi-Level WCAG 2.2 AA Adjustments: 3-step Text Size (+15%, +30%, +45%), 3-step Text Spacing, 3-step Line Height (1.75x, 2.0x, 2.25x), Text Alignment, Dyslexia-friendly typography, Highlight Headings, Highlight Links, Enhanced Keyboard Focus Ring, horizontal Reading Mask, Reading Guide, High Contrast, Invert Colors, Saturation (Low, High, Monochrome), Hide Images, Big Cursor, and Pause Animations.

4. Physical Restaurant Accessibility, Drive-Thru Assistance & Service Animals

Our commitment to accessibility extends across all 15 Tacos Gavilan brick-and-mortar restaurants in Southern California pursuant to ADA Title III (28 C.F.R. Part 36), the California Unruh Civil Rights Act (Cal. Civ. Code § 51), and the California Disabled Persons Act (Cal. Civ. Code §§ 54–54.1):

  • On-Site Restaurant Facilities: Our restaurants feature designated ADA accessible parking spaces (where on-site parking lots are provided), step-free accessible routes and entrances, accessible ordering counters, accessible dining room seating, and accessible restrooms.
  • Drive-Thru Alternative Accommodations (6 Drive-Thru Locations): At our 6 locations equipped with Drive-Thru lanes (including our Los Angeles (Central & Washington) restaurant at 1900 S. Central Ave.), guests with mobility, visual, hearing, or speech disabilities—including wheelchair or non-motorized guests unable to use a vehicular drive-thru lane when dining rooms close late at night—may call the restaurant directly upon arrival (or dial 711 via California Relay Service) so a team member can provide safe, prompt order taking and assisted window or curbside service.
  • Trained Service Animals Always Welcome: Individually trained service animals (as defined under ADA Title III, 28 C.F.R. § 36.104, and California Civil Code § 54.1) accompanying guests with disabilities are always welcome at no extra charge in all public dining and ordering areas of all 15 Tacos Gavilan locations.

5. Known Limitations & Third-Party Platforms

While we engineer and test our first-party pages on tacosgavilan.com directly, certain external ordering portals, point-of-sale integrations, delivery marketplaces, map canvases, and social media channels linked from our Site are operated by independent third parties outside of Tacos Gavilan's source-code control:

  • Online Ordering Storefront (order.online operated by DoorDash, Inc.) & Toast POS (Toast, Inc.): Clicking "Order Online" directs you to https://order.online, maintained by DoorDash, Inc. and connected to our restaurants' Toast Point-of-Sale (POS) system. If you encounter any barrier using order.online or Toast checkout flows with your assistive technology, please call any of our restaurants directly (such as (424) 785-5535 or (213) 742-6677, or via 711 Relay) so a team member can read menu items and prices and place a telephone pick-up order for you.
  • Third-Party Delivery Marketplaces (Uber Eats, DoorDash, Grubhub, and Postmates): External mobile apps and websites operated by Uber Eats, DoorDash, Grubhub, and Postmates are governed by each platform's own accessibility program and terms. Our team is always happy to assist directly by phone during store hours.
  • Interactive Map Canvas (Google Maps Platform) & External Social Media: Embedded Google Maps canvases and external social pages (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) may exhibit third-party keyboard or screen-reader limitations. To guarantee independent access without using the map widget, complete street addresses, cross-streets, hours, clickable tel: numbers, and directions links for all 15 locations are provided in static HTML cards on our Homepage and /locations/.

6. 24/7 Barrier Reporting, Direct Telephone Assistance, 711 Relay & 1–2 Business Day SLA

Consistent with California accessibility standards (including Thurston v. Midvale Corp.), if you encounter any digital or physical accessibility barrier, need information in an alternative accessible format, or want live human assistance reviewing our menu or placing a telephone pick-up order, please reach us free of charge through any of the following channels:

  • 24/7 Accessibility Support Email: support@tacosgavilan.com (accepts accessibility reports and alternative-format requests 24 hours a day, 7 days a week).
  • 24/7 Online Accessibility Form (Pre-Selected): Visit https://tacosgavilan.com/contact/?reason=accessibility, which automatically selects "Website Accessibility / ADA Assistance" as the inquiry topic, and receive an immediate on-screen Confirmation Reference ID. Helpful details include the page URL, a brief description of the issue, and the assistive technology or browser used.
  • Direct Live Telephone Assistance (Click-to-Call): For immediate human assistance during daily restaurant operating hours, call our Lynwood Guest Support & Restaurant Line at (424) 785-5535, our Los Angeles (Central & Washington) Drive-Thru location at (213) 742-6677, Los Angeles (Vernon & Broadway) at (323) 233-2302, Los Angeles (Slauson & Broadway) at (323) 615-0084, or view direct telephone numbers and operating hours for all 15 Southern California locations at /locations/.
  • California Relay Service (711): Guests who are deaf, hard of hearing, deaf-blind, or have speech disabilities may dial 711 at any time (free 24/7 California Relay Service in English and Spanish) to connect with any Tacos Gavilan restaurant.
  • Written Postal Mail Notice Address: Tacos Gavilan — Accessibility & Guest Support, 5182 Malabar Street, Vernon, CA 90058.
  • Response Timeframe Commitment (1–2 Business Days SLA): Every email or online accessibility report is logged with a tracking Reference ID and routed directly to our web engineering and operations leadership. We acknowledge and respond to accessibility assistance and barrier reports within 1 to 2 business days (and provide immediate live assistance by phone during restaurant operating hours).

Need Immediate Assistance or Want to Report an Accessibility Barrier?

Email support@tacosgavilan.com (24/7, 1–2 business days response), use our pre-selected Accessibility Assistance Form, call (424) 785-5535 / any of our 15 locations during operating hours, or dial 711 (California Relay Service). Written mail: Tacos Gavilan — Accessibility & Guest Support, 5182 Malabar Street, Vernon, CA 90058.
