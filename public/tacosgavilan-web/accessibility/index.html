Accessibility Statement

Tacos Gavilan is committed to digital accessibility and ensuring our website is welcoming and accessible to all guests, including individuals with disabilities.

1. Conformance Standards & WCAG 2.2 AA Target

We target the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as our benchmark for digital accessibility. Our continuous testing covers keyboard navigation, high-contrast visual modes, text scaling, descriptive alternative text, logical focus management, and screen-reader semantics under Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and the California Unruh Civil Rights Act (Cal. Civ. Code § 51).

2. Accessible Ordering Alternatives & Telephone Support

If any portion of our digital experience presents a barrier, guests may place orders directly through Toast POS (order.online) or contact any of our 15 restaurant locations by phone for dedicated telephone ordering and dietary assistance.

3. Feedback & Contact Information

We actively welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our site. If you encounter any accessibility barrier or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us:

  • Email: info@tacosgavilan.com (Subject: "Website Accessibility")
  • Form: Online Contact Form
