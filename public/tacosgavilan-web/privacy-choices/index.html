Your California Privacy Choices — Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Last Updated: October 16, 2026. Pursuant to the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA § 1798.135), Tacos Gavilan provides this page so you can easily manage your cookie preferences and exercise your right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

Use the buttons below to immediately opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information (disabling advertising & remarketing tags) or to customize your cookie categories:

1. Your Right to Opt Out of "Sale" or "Sharing" (CCPA / CPRA § 1798.135)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of their personal information. Tacos Gavilan never sells your personal information for monetary payment. However, under California law, allowing third-party analytics and advertising partners (such as Google Ads / Google Tag Manager, Meta, or TikTok) to collect online device identifiers or browsing activity via cookies and pixels for cross-context behavioral advertising may be considered "sharing" personal information.

When you click "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" above (or toggle the option inside our Cookie Preference Center), we immediately record your opt-out preference, enforce Google Consent Mode v2 (setting ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization, and analytics_storage to denied), disable Google Analytics/Ads tracking, and purge non-essential tracking cookies from your browser.

2. Global Privacy Control (GPC) Automatic Recognition

In compliance with California CPRA regulations (11 CCR § 7025), our website automatically detects and honors the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal broadcast by your browser or privacy extension (navigator.globalPrivacyControl === true). When a GPC signal is detected, our website automatically treats it as a valid, frictionless opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information for that browser.

3. Essential Storage & Cookies We Use

Even when you opt out of analytics and advertising cookies, our website uses strictly necessary local storage keys so the site functions properly according to your preferences:

  • tacosgavilan_lang — Remembers your selected display language (English or Español).
  • tg_cookie_consent / tacosgavilan_cookie_consent — Remembers your California privacy and cookie consent status so your opt-out is enforced on every page.
  • tg_a11y_prefs — Remembers any ADA accessibility adjustments (such as high contrast or enlarged text) you enable.

4. How to Submit Requests to Know, Delete, or Correct

In addition to opting out of cookie/pixel sharing above, California residents may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information submitted through our website (such as contact form inquiries) through either of the following two designated methods:

  • By Email: info@tacosgavilan.com (Please include "California Privacy Request" in the subject line).
  • By Web Form: Submit a request through our Online Contact Form.

For full details on our data practices, please read our complete Privacy Policy & California Privacy Notice.
