Your Privacy Choices & California Consumer Notice

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information.

Notice at Collection & Sale / Sharing Practices

Tacos Gavilan does NOT sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Therefore, an opt-out mechanism for the sale or sharing of personal data is not required, as no such sale or sharing takes place.

Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We respect privacy signals. If your browser transmits a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, our site automatically honors your preference. Because we do not engage in targeted advertising or data sales, your browsing experience remains fully private by default.

How to Exercise Your California Rights

You have the right to request:

  • Right to Know: The categories and specific pieces of personal information collected about you.
  • Right to Delete: Deletion of personal information collected from you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to Correct: Correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: You will never receive discriminatory pricing, service levels, or penalties for exercising your privacy rights.

To submit a verifiable consumer request, contact us via any of these channels:

  • Email: info@tacosgavilan.com (Subject: "CCPA Consumer Request")
  • Form: Contact Us Form
