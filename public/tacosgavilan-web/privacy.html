Privacy Policy & California Privacy Notice (CCPA/CPRA)

Effective and last updated October 2026. Tacos Gavilan explains here what this website collects, why it is used, and the choices available to California visitors.

1. Information We Collect

Our hosting and security infrastructure processes technical request data including IP address, browser User Agent, timestamp, and language preference to securely deliver site content. Vercel hosts this website; Supabase manages contact inquiries securely; Toast POS handles online orders; Google provides typography and interactive restaurant maps.

2. Contact Form Inquiries

When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, optional phone number, preferred restaurant location, topic, and message solely to respond to your inquiry and prevent automated spam abuse.

3. Data Retention Criteria

Contact messages and inquiries are retained for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purpose for which they were collected, resolve customer inquiries, or comply with applicable legal, tax, or regulatory obligations, after which they are securely deleted or de-identified.

4. Do Not Sell or Share Personal Information

Tacos Gavilan does NOT sell your personal information or share it with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not place third-party marketing tracking cookies. Google Maps loads only when requested to locate your nearest restaurant; Toast POS processes online orders securely on its dedicated platform.

5. Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information, and will not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights.

To exercise any California privacy rights, you may submit a request through our Contact Form or email us at info@tacosgavilan.com. You may also view our dedicated Privacy Choices page.
