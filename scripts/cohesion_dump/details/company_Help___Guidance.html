Adding New Workflows

  Open In New Tab   Print

Follow the Steps below to add a new Workflow to Cohesion - they'll walk you through everything you need to select and gather, without affecting any Workflow you're already using.

Step 1 · Select what integration you want to implement with Cohesion.

Not sure what these terms mean?

  • Workflow is what you want to send - your daily Sales, Time Entries (hours worked), or a Payroll Journal.
  • Source is the point-of-sale (POS) system you use in your restaurant, like Toast or Qu.
  • Target is the accounting or payroll software you want that information sent to, like QuickBooks or ADP.
